Frequently asked

Is this connected to real machines?

No. It is a concept prototype with fictional demo data and simulated telemetry.

Which processes is it designed around?

Injection molding first, plus assembly and packaging, with OEE, SPC, maintenance, quality and continuous improvement modules.

Are the AI suggestions engineering instructions?

No. They are demonstration content. Process changes need validated trials and qualified engineers.

Can it be customized for a consultancy?

That is the idea of the concept: a client-ready workspace for assessments, projects and deliverables.
